## LOCATION & FARM
- **Barangay New Eden, Pangantucan, Bukidnon, Philippines**, on the slopes/foothills of **Mt. Kalatungan**, Northern Mindanao.
- **Elevation: 1,300–1,400 MASL** (meters above sea level). (SunStar says 1,400; Good Cup says 1,300–1,400. Use "1,300–1,400 MASL".)
- **Established 2020** — planted during the pandemic. (Matches the logo's "EST 2020".)
- Cool growing climate, around **14°C**.
- **Shade-grown under Falcata trees** (a nitrogen-fixing legume that shields the coffee from direct sun).
- **Certified organic, sustainable** operations.
- Everything done **by hand**: planting, sorting, hand-washing, fermenting, sun-drying with manual turning.
- Key infrastructure: a **nursery** (built before the first harvest), **raised drying beds**, and a **fermentation processing station**.

## VARIETALS (Coffee page) — 5 varieties
1. **Bourbon** — delicately handled; double-sorted; stored at 16–18°C; soft, mild profile.
2. **Sarchimor** — disease-resistant arabica hybrid.
3. **Typica** — classic heirloom arabica.
4. **Catimor** — a Timor × Caturra cross; grown with wide spacing; shown in a **carbonic** process lot.
5. **"Sweet Coffee"** — an **Ethiopian heirloom landrace**, **confirmed by World Coffee Research DNA testing**; naturally sweet, shade-grown; so clean it can be mistaken for Ethiopian coffee in a blind tasting. Ethiopian genetics are "known for resilience, flavor complexity, and climate adaptability."
