Recognition

Nationally Recognized.

In only a handful of harvests, Finca de Garces has earned three consecutive years of national recognition at the Philippine Coffee Quality Competition — including the country’s Top 1 Arabica Estate Farm. Not by chasing trophies, but by tending to every detail of the craft with patience, honesty, and genuine love for the land.

what recognition means to us

What Recognition Means to Us

We do not display these recognitions as trophies on a shelf. Each one is a quiet confirmation that the way we grow — slowly, sustainably, with deep respect for the process — is a path worth continuing. Awards do not change how we farm; they affirm that the patience behind every cup is worth it. They belong just as much to the farmers, the land, and the community that makes this possible as they do to us.

grounded excellence

Quality Recognized Nationally

At the 2025 Philippine Coffee Quality Competition, our highland lot was named the country’s Top 1 Arabica Estate Farm — the judges noting caramel, orange-peel zest, floral notes, and a tea-like finish. Recognition like this belongs to the land, the farmers, and the patience behind every harvest. We invite you to taste that story for yourself.
