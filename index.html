who we are

A Family’s Purpose, Grown in the Highlands

Finca de Garces began as an act of faith during the pandemic — a family farm in the highlands of Pangantucan, Bukidnon, built on resilience, love for the land, and a deep desire to uplift the community around them. What started as a quiet survival project has grown into a nationally awarded specialty coffee farm, rooted in stewardship and the belief that great coffee can change lives. We grow slowly, process patiently, and share everything we learn.

what we offer

Specialty Coffee, Grown with Care

From our highland farm in Bukidnon, we offer carefully grown specialty coffee, experimentally nursed seedlings, and hands-on farm consulting — each an expression of our commitment to quality and community.

Specialty Coffee

Green Beans & Roasted

Our highland-grown beans are selected at peak ripeness and processed with patience, preserving the complex, clean flavors the Bukidnon terroir imparts. Available as specialty-grade green beans or roasted to order, each lot carries the story of the soil, the elevation, and the hands that tended it.

Seedlings

Experimentally Nursed Seedlings

We cultivate experimental coffee varieties with the same slow intentionality we bring to every part of the farm, nurturing each seedling through careful observation and research. Our nursery program makes quality planting material available to fellow farmers who want to grow with purpose.

Consulting

Farm Consulting

We walk alongside fellow farmers — sharing knowledge, practices, and honest experience — because we believe improving quality in one farm lifts the entire community. Our consulting is rooted in humility and a genuine desire to see Philippine specialty coffee grow from the ground up.

Our Story

Born of Resilience. Rooted in Purpose.

When the world shut down, one family turned to their land — not out of desperation alone, but out of faith that something meaningful could grow from the highlands of Bukidnon. That quiet beginning became a nationally recognized specialty coffee farm, not by rushing, but by staying true to the soil, the community, and the patient craft behind every bean.

what drives us

Rooted in These Values

Resilience & Patience

We tend the land through every season, trusting that slow, steady care brings the truest harvest.

Community & Kinship

We grow alongside our neighbors, believing that lifting other farmers and communities is inseparable from our own purpose.

Faith & Purpose

Our farm was born in faith — the conviction that meaningful work, done with integrity, finds its way to the people it was meant for.

Excellence Without Rush

Every step — from planting to processing — is done with care and without haste, because quality cannot be hurried.

Stewardship of Land

We treat the soil, the water, and the forest not as resources to consume but as a living inheritance to protect and pass on.

Authenticity

We tell our story honestly — the struggles, the learning, and the wins — because the people behind the coffee matter as much as the cup itself.

recognized for excellence
